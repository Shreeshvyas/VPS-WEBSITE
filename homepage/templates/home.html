Frequently Asked Questions

FAQS

What is the admission process at Vyas Public School?

Admission forms can be collected from the school office or downloaded from our website. Entrance tests and interviews may be required for certain classes.

What documents are required for admission?

Required documents typically include:

  • Birth certificate
  • Aadhar card copy
  • Previous school report card (if applicable)
  • Passport-sized photographs
  • Transfer certificate (for Grades 2 and above)

When do admissions start?

Started from 1st July.

What curriculum does Vyas Public School follow?

We follow the (Central Board of Secondary Education) curriculum.
