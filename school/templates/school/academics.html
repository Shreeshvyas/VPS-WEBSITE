Our Academic Structure

Our curriculum is customized to foster academic rigour, problem solving, creative thinking, and moral strength at every level.

Primary Wing (Grades I - V)

Focuses on basic literacy, numeracy, experiential activities, environmental studies, and creative expression through music and art.

  • Play-based learning
  • Basic arithmetic & language
  • Art & craft activities

Middle Wing (Grades VI - VIII)

Transitioning to structured sciences, social studies, mathematics, computer programming, and dual languages with sports training.

  • Science & Math foundation
  • Computer programming labs
  • Competitive sports training

Senior Wing (Grades IX - XII)

Specialized streams (Science, Commerce, Agriculture) prepared for college entrance, laboratory research projects, and leadership roles.

  • MP.BOARD / Board exam preparation
  • Physics, Chemistry, Biology labs
  • Programming Languages Guidance (Python, Java, C++)
  • Career counselling guides
