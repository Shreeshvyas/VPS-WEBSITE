Our Pillars of Strength

Our Illustrious Founders & Visionaries

Establishing Vyas Public School in 1995, their ultimate dedication and visionary leadership laid the cornerstone of our academic legacy, nurturing it into a beacon of modern education.

Sahitya Ratan • Rashtrapati Awardee
Founder & Visionary

Shri Nandkishore Vyas (N.K. Vyas)

B.Sc M.A. (Hindi + Pol. Science) Literature English B.Ed.

A distinguished scholar, prolific litterateur, and visionary educator, Shri Nandkishore Vyas was honored by the President of India with the prestigious 'Sahitya Ratan'. His deep academic expertise and passion for language and literature shaped the educational ethos of Vyas Public School. Under his guidance, the institution pioneered holistic development, bridging traditional values with scientific inquiry.

Founder & Pillar of Strength

Smt. Shushila Devi Vyas

B.A. (Hindi) M.A. (Hindi) B.Ed.

Co-establishing the school in 1995, Smt. Shushila Devi Vyas has been the cornerstone of the school's growth. With her academic credentials in language education, she spearheaded administrative excellence and created a nurturing environment. Her dedication to the welfare of teachers and students alike has built a legacy of warmth, discipline, and high moral integrity.

Established in 1995, the journey of Vyas Public School was paved by the untiring efforts, passion, and deep wisdom of our founders. Together, they did not just open a school; they created a temple of learning. Every milestone we achieve today stands on the solid foundation of excellence they built. We owe the current heights and prestige of this institution to their everlasting credit and vision.
